# Research snapshot scope

This subtree contains research code, tests, finite initial model coordinates,
protocols, small numerical charge tables and reference metadata. Original
publication data archives, the thesis, presentation, Gaussian binaries,
checkpoints and live campaign logs are retained outside Git. Source manifests
refer to the full local scientific archive; not all listed original artifacts
are bundled here. Job submission is not completed or validated science.

The four commercial-material quick presets belong to the builder, not to this
finite-fragment fitting study. No new final force-field library is published
by this initial research snapshot.
