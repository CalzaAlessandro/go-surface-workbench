Protocol and limitations of the fit

Reference: HF/6-31G* MK and external Amber RESP on accepted ωB97X-D/6-311++G(d,p) geometries. External RHF instability is not erased and a stable RHF wavefunction is not claimed. Training: seven C16 contexts. Test: seven supported C24 contexts; EPH/EPT excluded, not filled artificially. Predefined default: hardness 4, bond hardness 10, width scale 1, ridge 0.0001; only role electronegativities fitted. The 11 sensitivity configurations are exploratory.

The solver is verified against three native ChargeFW results and with conservation, permutation, bond orientation, rotation, and COO resonance tests. These numerical checks do not prove physical transferability or adsorption quality. Published NPA coefficients were not used as RESP coefficients.

Presets and next selection criterion

GAFF2–AIM and HierGO/OPLS remain indivisible bundles of charges, bonded terms, LJ, mixing, and 1–4 conventions. Their tables are not converted into generic GAFF2 charges. GAFF2–AIM does not automatically cover edges and carboxylates; COO⁻ is missing from the archived HierGO table. Full generic mapping and Amber conversion remain to be validated. The catalog describes this policy; it does not yet implement a universal selector.

I would keep L0 as a simple baseline. With the new references, I would compare A: fixed library; B: conservative classes or increments by graph neighborhood; C: SQE, also verifying a direct objective on the potential with RESP regularization. Entire contexts must be excluded from fitting, including carboxylates, distances, and sizes; do not choose settings on the test set. Comparison with LJ/adsorption comes after the provisional choice of charges.

APBS: useful after assignment

APBS starts from assigned charges and radii and computes electrostatics and solvent/ion response in a continuum model. It can show model sensitivity to salt and solvation, but does not alone provide an independent reference to derive or validate those same charges. For our goal, HF–RESP remains primary; full QM monitors electronic and scaling effects. We do not fit Amber charges to a PB potential already screened by solvent.
