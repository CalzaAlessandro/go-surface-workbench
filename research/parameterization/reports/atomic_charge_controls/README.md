# Finite atomic charge controls

These seven atom-mapped charge sets are computed AM1-BCC comparison controls
for the finite fragment graphs. Raw native charges and the explicitly derived
integer-charge constrained-rounding candidate are retained side by side.
They are not transferable GO residue libraries or final production parameters.
The dione is excluded for incomplete explicit BCC coverage and is documented
separately. Raw logs and the 161 verified artifacts remain in the local/HPC
campaign archive; reports record scope and original source hashes.
