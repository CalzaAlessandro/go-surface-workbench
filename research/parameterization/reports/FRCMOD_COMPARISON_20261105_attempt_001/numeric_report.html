Limitations and interpretation

constraint
The DAT and historical loading order of the local files are unknown; the bases are candidate comparators, not demonstrated provenance.
Comparisons between identical signatures do not establish identity of the chemical environment. The AMBER namespace is excluded from cross-comparison with GAFF.
Wildcards and improper permutations screen candidates; actual LEaP precedence, graph matches, resolved topologies, 1–4 exclusions, and QM validation are not simulated.
A frcmod without MASS/BOND/NONBON can inherit everything from the base: absent rows are not automatically missing parameters.
A zero torsional term can be a conventional choice; an analogy penalty is not an energy error and does not measure accuracy.
Suffixed AIM types are environment classes and do not automatically reduce to GAFF types. Ethers and lactones require explicit graphs and typing; os/op alone do not establish their coverage.
