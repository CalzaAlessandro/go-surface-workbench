GO charges: extended comparison of BCC, ABCG2, and NAGL

November 5, 2026 · results completed on fragments, no replacement of production libraries.

Result: NAGL passes technical checks on all 16 fragments. BCC and ABCG2 have verified coverage on 12/16 each. On these fragments, ABCG2 reproduces the QM potential less closely than classic BCC. This comparison does not measure the accuracy of solvation free energies, which was the target of the ABCG2 parameterization.

Coverage: both native methods were excluded for pyrene_dione, C24_single_basal_EPX_control, C24_EPH_edge_control, and C24_OH_OH_near_trans. The bond-charge correction between BCC types 12 and 16 with bond order 2 is missing. The missing entry is explicitly rejected: it is not replaced with zero.

BCC protocol: the same archived AM1 populations are reused; the table and native typing of corrections change. Raw native charges are preserved. For scoring, a separate integer-total control is used, correcting only the residual from rounding AM1 populations to three decimal places. The correction is reported for each atom; it is not a new parameterization.

NAGL protocol: openff-gnn-am1bcc-1.0.0 model, openff-nagl 0.6.1, PyTorch 2.7.1. Atom map, connectivity, total charge, repeatability, atom permutation, and carboxylate resonance representation checked. No subsequent renormalization of charges. Passing these checks does not prove transferability to GO.

Full sheets GOX935 and REGOX980 remain excluded from NAGL assignment: valence graphs and electronic states are not yet established. Counts of unresolved carbon sites are graph diagnostics, not calculated physical multiplicities. No hydrogens were added and sheets were not altered to force the model.

How to read the scientific result

HF/6-31G* is here the conventional branch already calculated, retaining the known instability of the RHF determinant. The second potential is ωB97XD/6-311++G(d,p). The choice of QM reference is still under validation. Neither column identifies a unique atomic charge or alone proves accuracy in MD.

L0 and SQE remain promising for the electrostatics of this panel. NAGL improves substantially over the tested Espaloma model, but does not outperform the original LIB on the medians of this panel. ABCG2 is an important control for a different fitting objective: solution properties and reproduction of gas-phase potential require distinct verifications.

RESP or ABCG2 for small molecules?

RESP is supported with both GAFF and GAFF2. ABCG2 is the BCC variant optimized for GAFF2. In the published 2025 comparison across 642 neutral FreeSolv solutes, the hydration free energy RMSE is 0.99 kcal/mol for GAFF2/ABCG2, 1.48 for GAFF2/RESP, and 1.71 for GAFF2/AM1-BCC. FreeSolv is also an optimization target: these numbers are not entirely an external validation. The comparison does not establish superiority for GO, ions, or GBSA.

For the upcoming pollutants database, GAFF2 + ABCG2 is noted as a candidate, with GAFF2 + RESP as a comparison. No retroactive changes: operational note for the next phase.

Comparison with Stauffer and preserved parameters

Stauffer ESP and SESP are not RESP. SESP scales periodic ESP charges by 1.5 for the water model context. Our test adds finite LIB edges, absent in the periodic parameterization: this is an explicitly stated hybrid control. Agreement of the unscaled model with a gas-phase potential does not demonstrate that it is better in water.

The seven original frcmod files from Hoshin, AmberGO, OLD, and current are preserved byte-for-byte with an SHA256 manifest in HOSHIN_OLD_CURRENT_20261105. Comparison and selection of bonded/LJ parameters remain downstream of the charge phase.
