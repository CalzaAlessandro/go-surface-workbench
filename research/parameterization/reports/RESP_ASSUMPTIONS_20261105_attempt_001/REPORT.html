4. Permissible scientific interpretation

  • The data allow verifying whether internal carbon parameters are zero, whether blocks respect a chosen local neutrality, and how much they vary with method and context.
  • A small group total may conceal cancellations among large atomic charges. A large RESP charge on an internal C does not alone demonstrate a physical transfer of electrons: the atomic partitioning derives from the ESP fitting model.
  • Poorly exposed carbons may be weakly identified by the external ESP. This audit does not compute fit conditioning, identifiability intervals, or a partitioned electron density; large values are not validated as atomic observables.
  • Distance tables and group pairs do not causally separate distance, face, geometry, loading, edge, and size. They do not justify a transferable rule or universal cutoff radius beyond which to set q = 0.
  • HF fits maintain the caveat of unvalidated RHF determinant stability; accepted ground state is not asserted. DFT stability is reported from source metadata and not recalculated. RESP completion does not imply upstream QM gate approval.
  • No new QM calculation, fit, force field assignment, MD test, bulk property, or modular transferability was performed. It is not a production-ready parameterization.

5. Controls and reproducibility

Molecular totals, block sums, anchors, and graph distances were recalculated from local files; identity, order, atomic charges, and summary coordinates were compared against inputs. SHA-256 source hashes are in source_manifest.json. Comparison with hashes embedded in FIT_COMPLETE.json is kept separate in source_artifact_hash_gates.csv: any differences are preserved, without promoting metadata to method validation.

Statistical unit: one molecular fit. Thresholds of 0.05 and 0.10 e in the CSVs: descriptive, not physical acceptance criteria. No external scientific source is used to replace local data.
