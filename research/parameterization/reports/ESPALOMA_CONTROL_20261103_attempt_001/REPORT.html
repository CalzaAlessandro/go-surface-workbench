EspalomaCharge was run on 16 finite GO graphs. The comparison with native BCC uses the same 12 cases with complete BCC parameters; the four excluded cases remain explicit. HF–RESP remains the primary reference of the project.

BCC–library proximity does not imply equivalence with Espaloma

The library is closer to native BCC than Espaloma is on this panel. This is a difference between charge vectors, not a measure of physical accuracy. Espaloma approximates an AM1-BCC ELF10 protocol; the native control here comes from Amber/SQM. Protocol differences and domain transfer must be distinguished: this test alone does not establish the cause of the discrepancy.

For each molecule, the RMS deviation between corresponding atoms is calculated, followed by the median across contexts. Contexts carry equal weight. Uncovered BCC cases are not imputed with zero.

Total charge control: adapter required in the tested release

Release v0.0.8 calculates a total_charge value in the API, but does not forward it to the model; the featurizer stores formal_charge, while the readout uses q_ref. The acetate probe with the unadapted API returns a total near zero. In the benchmark, the q_ref field receives the formal charges already present in the graph: the published readout uses their sum. Weights are not refitted and the total is respected without posterior rescaling.

The model was loaded in inference mode; prediction repetition and atomic order permutation were recorded. This is a test of the published model with declared input adapter, not a certification of the standard API for charged GO.

  • Maximum deviation on formal total across the 16 cases: 9.09e-07 e.
  • Maximum difference upon repeating inference: 0 e.
  • Maximum difference after permutation and ID restoration: 5.96e-08 e.

Test limitations

The dataset contains finite fragments, not large sheets or proteins. The test confirms technical feasibility and conservation of total charge with the adapter; it does not validate adsorption, solvent, or LJ parameters. Eight contexts have inherited R/S metadata that do not match those derived from optimized coordinates. They are recorded without modifying the sources. Descriptors in this version do not include coordinates or stereochemistry; the benchmark does not constitute a validation of stereochemical labels.

Coupling with GAFF and Lennard-Jones

Charges and LJ jointly influence hydration, contacts, and adsorption. Bonded parameters may also depend on the electrostatic model with which they were developed. Studying charges first is useful to isolate hypotheses; final validation must keep the charge–LJ pair identified and verify relevant bonded parameters.

In GAFF2–AIM, bonded parameters come from GAFF2 parameterization of the fragments. From DFT density they obtain DDEC6 for charges and atomic volumes for LJ via the procedure described in the SI. They do not describe a new AIM bonded fit. The joint derivation of charges and LJ is not equivalent to a simultaneous fit of all parameters against experiments.

See the note on their coupling. This Espaloma control does not modify any GAFF, LJ, or frcmod parameter.

What SQE recalibration requires

  1. Define a small number of atom and bond families covering basal, oxidation, edge, and protonation.
  2. Use the already calculated molecular HF–RESP references; expand especially COOH/COO⁻ and mixed contexts. A single carboxylate molecule is not sufficient to validate transferability.
  3. Select a charge baseline and initially fix a subset of parameters. Estimate a few response coefficients with regularization before freeing all parameter families.
  4. Compare charges and generated potential on entire contexts excluded from fitting, verifying total charge, numerical stability, and size dependence.

An SQE set adapted to our RESP charges would be a new GO calibration. The original parameter sets from the SQE+qp paper use NPA references. The software and architecture can be reused; coefficients do not become suitable for our target simply by replacing initial charges.
