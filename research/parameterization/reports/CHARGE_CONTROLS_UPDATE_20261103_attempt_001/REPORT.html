Interpretation and working decision

For the epoxide in the mixed fragment, oxygen shifts from −0.370 e in the library to −0.138 e with Espaloma and −0.607 e with SQE+qp; molecular HF–RESP gives −0.419 e. The controls do not converge to a single distribution. The HF–RESP comparison concerns the entire fragment, with constraints different from a library of neutral blocks.

In carboxylate, SQE+q0 gives an average of −0.908 e per oxygen and SQE+qp −0.472 e. The fragment total remains −1 e in both: what changes is how much charge is localized on COO⁻ or distributed over the scaffold. The COO⁻ subset charge was not forced to −1 in the molecular references. This is precisely one of the cases to expand before choosing a transferable model.

I would retain Espaloma as a rapid control targeting AM1-BCC. For a future redistribution model calibrated for Amber, I would compare SQE+q0 and SQE+qp against our HF–RESP data, with validation on fragment families excluded from fitting. I would not directly adopt the published SQE coefficients as a RESP preset for GO.

Reproducibility: dependence on the Kekulé form

The native library requires integer bond orders. The validated aromatic graphs were kekulized with RDKit without changing atoms, hydrogens, geometry, connectivity or formal charge. A second equivalent representation, obtained by reversing atom order before kekulization, changes some single/double bond orders.

This variation changes an atomic charge by up to 0.0961 e for SQE+qp in carboxylate, 0.0451 e for SQE+q0 and 0.0580 e for standard SQE. Pristine sheets also show a smaller dependence. In contrast, atom permutation with the same bond orders and rigid rotation/translation pass the numerical checks. Consistent handling of aromaticity/resonance therefore needs to be defined and validated before this can become a general charge assigner.

Total-charge conservation: maximum error 2.46 × 10⁻¹⁴ e for SQE+qp; no manual correction applied to predictions. Charges would remain fixed during MD. The initial uniform redistribution of tabulated q0 values is part of the published SQE+qp formulation; it is neither a RESP correction nor use of your LIB as a baseline.

Methods, coverage and limitations

Native C++ implementation ChargeFW 0.1.7, distributed by the ChargeFW2 maintainer; Python 3.11 in an isolated environment. Explicit parameter sets SQE/SQEq0/SQEqp_Schindler2021_CCD_gen, strict matching, complete solution, no automatic selection or refitting. The published coefficients are calibrated against B3LYP/6-311G NPA, according to the set metadata and the original paper. These are not RESP charges.

Espaloma uses the published v0.0.8 weights and the readout's q_ref field to supply the formal total. The unadapted API in that release gave approximately 0 for acetate instead of −1; the control with the adapter preserves −1. Weights unchanged; 16/16 fragments, no new fit. The model targets AM1-BCC ELF10, which does not necessarily match the native Amber/SQM procedure in our control.

The 12 AM1-BCC comparisons retain the exclusions of the four cases lacking BCC parameters. Inconsistent inherited stereochemical metadata are preserved: neither stereochemistry nor transferability to large surfaces is declared validated. SQE uses a dense solution, with quadratic memory and a solution cost cubic in the number of bonds: fragment timings do not demonstrate linear scaling.
