GO-MACE: three different uses

ObjectivePossible use
Predict RESP chargesPublished GO-MACE does not provide this output. QM potentials/densities/dipoles or RESP labels are needed; energies and forces do not uniquely separate charges, LJ and bonded terms.
Derive GAFF parametersA new fit of energies/forces/curvatures may be possible after fixing charges and LJ, within the potential's domain and with independent QM verification. This is not a direct conversion of the model file into frcmod.
Calibrate SQE for AMBERGO-MACE can provide geometries for sampling. A curated subset is recalculated with the agreed electrostatic reference; SQE is calibrated against that reference while maintaining the chemically determined total Q.

The final surface retains fixed charges during MD/GBSA. Standard GO-MACE, developed for GO and transformations in vacuum, has not already been validated for the electrostatic distribution of COO⁻, sheets with different charges or GO–protein interactions in solution.
