Which QM verifications are needed before choosing GO parameters?

The available files allow a comparison of provenance and coverage. Deciding accuracy requires verifications on graphs and physical references; this does not imply immediately refitting the entire force field.

Proposed decision. Preserve the complete presets, resolve charges first, and use the numerical audit to select a few discriminating tests. Priority is the context of epoxides and sp²/sp³ junctions; then torsions, edges, and interactions with water/adsorbates. This note does not modify parameters, does not prepare new jobs, and does not attribute bonded/LJ validation to active charge campaigns.

Complete log of evidence, paths, and access limits. The calculation proposals below are our recommendations, distinct from published results.

DDEC6 is technically usable in Amber. The point is the physical validation of pairing with LJ, types, combination rules, exclusions, and 1–4 factors. There is no technical prohibition requiring RESP. The isolated replacement of q is a hybrid control and does not automatically preserve the performance of the published preset.

Intramolecular nonbonded terms also contribute to torsional surfaces: the link between charge model and bonded terms is documented methodologically by Cerutti et al., 2017, on proteins. It does not quantify the effect in our GO and does not impose a universal refit at every change of q.

2. Physical tests to distinguish from coefficient auditing

Concrete problemMost informative referenceWhat it can decide / limitation
Isolated epoxide: C–O, C–C, C–O–C, puckering, and sp²/sp³ junctionsAccepted geometry, Hessian, and QM modes; small positive and negative deformations of the C–C–O triangle.Measures equilibrium and local stiffness. Correctness is not identified solely from angles around 60°.
Distinct adjacent epoxidesGraph containing the true cx-cx-cx path, plus two local orientations when present in the material.If the three C atoms do not form a triangle, the three-C ring term is not justified by the type signature. First correct the chemical contract; then decide whether an analogue suffices or a fit is required.
Ring openingInspection of the QM structure and, only if the reaction is the subject of study, reaction/TS coordinates and appropriate electronic state.Fixed-topology GAFF and GAFF2 can describe limited deformations around the minimum. They do not produce physical bond breaking/formation or a reliable reaction barrier by changing only frcmod.
Basal planarity, curvature, and bendingPristine and oxidized fragments of comparable size; out-of-plane modes and controlled bending deformations.A finite Hessian verifies local/improper stiffness. Sheet flexural rigidity and size effects subsequently require convergence checks on larger fragments or periodic reference; do not impose planarity on sp³ GO.
OH and group rotationsRelaxed scans of C–O–H and C–C(=O)OH anchoring; adjacent OH conformers with and without internal H-bonding.Minima, barriers, periodicity, and couplings. Compare total MM energy and components with stated q/LJ/1–4; the isolated torsional coefficient is not the full barrier.
Carbonyl, COOH, and COO⁻Separate references for protonation/conjugation; C=O, equivalence of the two carboxylate O atoms, improper of the center, and torsion relative to the edge.Hessian/geometry discriminate local errors. Solvation and ions must be verified separately, especially for COO⁻; a gas-phase anion does not prove aqueous behavior.
Water, ions, adsorbatesInteraction curves across multiple orientations and distances; then MD with independent observables and sampling uncertainties.Tests the q–LJ pair and contacts. Hessians of isolated GO and parmchk do not validate hydration, adsorption affinity, or wettability.

The modified Seminario method of Allen, Payne, and Cole is a possible approach for extracting bond/angle parameters from the Hessian. It is a methodological candidate to verify for Amber harmonic normalization, symmetry, and context; it does not derive LJ, does not replace torsional scans, and is not applied here.

3. First reuse what has already been calculated

The local report of October 30, dft_bonded_reference_QA_20261030.json, documents seven preliminary positive controls out of eight and no final validation of parameters. It includes pristine, cis/trans diols, diol–epoxide, dione, COOH, and COO⁻ at ωB97X-D/6-311++G(d,p). This is historical evidence from the report: raw Gaussian/fchk files were not retrieved again in this audit.

The case to resolve first is pyrene_epoxide. The report indicates the expected bond 15–16 at 2.11047 Å and a failed connectivity check, despite positive frequencies and a stable wavefunction. Before extracting constants, identify those atoms and verify the graph, bond order, epoxide position, final structure, and boundaries. Geometric cutoff alone does not demonstrate true epoxide opening. A minimum on a different connectivity is not a reference for the initial motif.

Re-reading acceptable geometries and associated Hessians is the most cost-effective initial check. If the material includes separate adjacent epoxides, explicitly verify that the existing pool contains that context: the diol–epoxide model does not replace it.

5. Minimum verification package, in order of cost

These quantities are an initial adaptive plan, not a CPU estimate and not calculations already performed. The numerical audit must first indicate which terms differ and to which real paths they apply.

PriorityProposed verificationMinimum cost / criterion to proceed
P0 · nowAssociate terms and atoms on complete topologies; total q, graphs, namespaces, base DAT, LJ, impropers, 1–4, and precedences. Examine existing QM references and the epoxide case.Zero new QM calculations. Distinguishes assignment errors from physical issues; resolve typing before fitting a constant.
P1 · reuseAccepted existing geometries and Hessians: QM/MM comparison with each preset separately. Projections onto C–O/C–C, ring modes, out-of-plane, and carbonyls; unconstrained MM minimizations that avoid hiding errors.Zero new QM if artifacts prove intact. Compare frequencies and modes via overlap, not just list order; exclude translations/rotations and state any scaling.

P3 · torsionsTwo initial relaxed scans: OH C–O and COOH–edge. Use an already present OH/epoxide context to verify coupling.12 points at 30° per scan, then refinement near minima/barriers; 2D scans only if coupling makes the result ambiguous. Decide the fit after selecting q/LJ.

For each: geometry/state, charge reference with chosen protocol, Hessian, and appropriate torsion, plus water probe. No simple reuse of COOH/EPX charges.
P4 · nonbondedFour initial GO–water curves: epoxide O acceptor, OH donor, OH acceptor, π region. P5 · transferabilityMD comparison on same material and sampling, with each complete preset and its own water/ion context. Observables: geometry/curvature, water structure, and adsorption with estimated error.Define replicates and convergence prior to ranking. Experiments on consistent material for O/C, groups, protonation, and hydration; any MD contacts/angles alone are not an experimental affinity.

For comparisons, preserve the effective 1–4 factors. In the published GAFF2-AIM preset: arithmetic combination of σ and geometric combination of ε, LJ factor 0.5, and Coulomb 0.8333. A comparison of charges alone at fixed bonded/LJ parameters addresses a distinct question from comparing full presets. Aqueous validation may favor an effective model over the closest gas-phase agreement: both results should be recorded, without compensating for typing errors with ad hoc charges or torsions.

6. Decision criterion before a refit

An existing parameter can be retained if its context is correct and reproduces the required local geometries and curvatures within criteria defined before the comparison. A targeted refit becomes warranted when a reproducible error remains after ruling out graph, protonation, units, basis/version, precedences, and nonbonded treatment. A type collision primarily requires distinguishing contexts; a difference between historical DAT files does not alone prove an error.

Work can proceed immediately on inventory, mapping, topologies, existing references, and defining the new ether/lactone motifs. The choice of charges, final parameterization, and selection of a winning model for adsorption remain open; none follows automatically from parmchk, an existing Hessian, or a completed job.
