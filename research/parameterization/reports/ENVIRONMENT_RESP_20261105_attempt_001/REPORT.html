L0 uses shared roles. L1 distinguishes the environment of group carbons via the roles of their nearest neighbors; O/H inherit the group context. Numbering, model name, coordinates, orientation, and resonance representation do not enter the key. COO oxygens remain equivalent. The tests verify these invariants.

To isolate the contribution of classes, both variants keep basal carbon at zero, neutral blocks or COO −1, and shared C–H edges. This controlled choice does not prove that these constraints are physically exact. Charges were obtained from the already verified external Amber RESP executable; the new L0 reproduces the previous fit with a maximum difference of 0.000000 e.

What molecular RESP says about the constraints

QuestionDescriptive result
Do internal carbons have q = 0?Not in the available fits: max |q| = 0.565372 e (DFT, C24_OH_EPX_near_trans, C11, d = 1). Pristine sheets also show non-zero internal charges.
Does |q| always decrease with d?No: mean |q| per shell is not monotonically non-increasing in 22/24 contexts with at least two internal distances. Descriptive count on dependent samples.
Does the complete COO carry −1 e?HF: carboxyl −0,766142 + anchor +0,086049 = −0,680093 e. DFT: carboxyl −0,760300 + anchor +0,114968 = −0,645332 e. The remaining −0,319908/−0,354667 e is on the rest of the model.
How much does the HF/DFT fit change?Across the 16 paired models, the atomic mean |Δq| ranges from 0.005581 to 0.044438 e per model; the maximum atomic |Δq| is 0.305283 e (C24_OH_EPX_near_trans).

RESP atomic charges are not unique observables. Poorly exposed carbons may be weakly identified by the potential; non-zero values in finite fragments are not sufficient to invalidate a library with zero baseline. Potentials, dipole moments, constraint checks, and transfer to larger sizes are needed. The observed non-monotonicity does not rule out a distance effect; it rules out the assumption that a single decreasing function is already proven.

Size, oxidation, and missing groups

Final validation must cross-check sheets of approximately 4–10 nm with low/high oxidation, isolated/clustered groups, and edge contexts. Comparisons with a fixed number of groups on larger fragments also alter coverage; they do not by themselves prove size transferability. Acceptance criteria and current limitations.

The SQE prototype uses dense matrices: cost and size scaling must be verified before proposing it for large sheets. For L0/L1, coverage of new classes and assignment validation separate from the limits of RESP input are required. No method is yet declared validated at 10 nm with high oxidation.

Non-epoxide ethers are documented in HierGO sources. Lactone lacks a dedicated verified parameterization in those sources: lactone (cyclic ester) and lactol (cyclic hemiacetal) enter as distinct references. Catalog of new references; no new charge or final parameter is assigned to these groups.

frcmod comparison and calculations still required

Verification of coefficients and their parameter baselines is separate from the charge comparison. A frcmod is a set of overrides and must be resolved with DAT baseline, atom types, and loading order. Coincidences with GAFF/GAFF2 document provenance, not physical validation.

  • Evidence and priorities for QM checks: reuse valid Hessians, verify topologies first, then introduce targeted checks on genuine missing contexts. No global refit or additional QM job for frcmod files was initiated here.
